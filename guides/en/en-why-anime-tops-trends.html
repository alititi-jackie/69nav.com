"Hentai" at #1 for Five Years Running

The more striking statistic is "hentai" holding the overall #1 search term for five straight years. Pornhub attributes this to the continued global growth of anime-adjacent culture — from video game characters and anime-inspired cosplay to fully rendered 3D animation.

In other words, the audience for animated adult content is not a niche subculture anymore; it is a durable, mainstream-scale global audience that keeps growing year after year.

Why Animation? The Cultural Engine

Several forces feed the trend:

  • Anime's mainstream boom — streaming platforms made anime a global prime-time medium in the 2020s, expanding the total audience familiar with the aesthetic.
  • Gaming crossovers — game-inspired characters and cosplay bridge gaming fandom and adult search behavior.
  • Stylization — animation offers fantasy scenarios and character designs impossible in live action, which is precisely the appeal for many viewers.
  • Community infrastructure — fan art sites, conventions, and creator platforms give the aesthetic a year-round cultural home beyond any single tube site.

Asia's Search Habits: Anime, Cosplay and Gaming

Regional data adds texture. Trend analyses of Asian markets consistently show "adult video" and "sex videos" dominating overall, with "anime sex" and "Japanese adult content" emerging as distinctive regional favorites — reflecting anime's deep roots in Japanese pop culture.

Japan ranked #11 among countries by traffic in 2025, and searches like "Japanese" charted in the global 11–30 range — further evidence of how Japanese visual culture punches above its weight in global adult search.

What the Trend Means

For platforms, the message is that animation is a first-class content vertical, not a novelty category — merchandising, creator tools, and recommendation systems increasingly reflect that. For viewers, it is a reminder that search data, aggregated anonymously, is one of the clearest windows into real cultural shifts: in this case, the full mainstreaming of anime aesthetics.

As with all Year in Review data, treat rankings as directional rather than precise — methodologies change year to year, and search terms are an imperfect proxy for viewing.

Yes — Asian market analyses show anime-adjacent terms among the most distinctive regional searches, and Japan itself is a top-15 traffic country.
